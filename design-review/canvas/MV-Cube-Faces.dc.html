The Cube in the sidebar

It sits above the Cube selector and fills most of the sidebar's width. Four faces, one for each place you can be, and it turns a quarter turn at a time as Mobile's Cube does: the face you are on is lit at the front left, the next one shows at the right.

How it behaves

  • Order. Overview, Wallets, Activity, Settings. The last three are Mobile's Spending, Activity and Settings faces, in the same order.
  • Turning. Picking a sidebar item turns the Cube to its face, the short way round, in 0.8 s with Mobile's easing. No turn with reduced motion.
  • Clicking a face goes to that place, so the face at the right is a one-click step forward.
  • Top face. The network coin, turned to face you.
  • Size. A 104 px Cube in the 248 px sidebar; the block is 178 px tall.
  • Not on a face yet. Marketplace (P2P, Buy/Sell). To decide.
